## About the Game

Puking Cat turns a familiar cat-owner problem into the whole point of the game.

The cat is not sick by accident. It is trying to cause damage.

Players aim the puke at furniture, food, plants, books and anything else within reach. The more objects you hit, the more damage you cause and the better your score.

Levels are short and replayable, with a three-star score to chase. Players earn Puke Coins as they play and can spend them on cat costumes and different puke palettes.

The game is built around a steady stream of new rooms and seasonal themes. The plan is to release three new levels per day, with new environments, object layouts and things to ruin throughout the year.

All levels are free to play with ads. An optional subscription removes the ads without locking any gameplay behind a paywall.

## Why Puking Cat Exists

The idea came from real life with our cats.

We used to joke that whenever one of them puked, it almost felt intentional. Not just on the floor. On the rug. The blanket. The one thing in the room you really did not want covered in cat puke.

That became the game: a cat trying to cause as much damage as possible in a room.

The idea sat in my Apple Notes for years before I finally started building it.

---

## What Makes It Different

Puking Cat is not a general cat simulator and it is not a game about cleaning up after a pet.

The joke is much narrower: cats somehow manage to puke on exactly the things you wish they would avoid.

That becomes the core mechanic. Instead of knocking objects around at random, the player tries to spread the mess across the room and hit as many objects as possible.

The puke itself is also part of the collection system, with different colors, themes and particles, alongside cat costumes.

## Content Plan

Puking Cat is designed as an ongoing game rather than a fixed set of rooms.

The content plan calls for three new levels per day, with themes following the calendar across autumn, Halloween, winter, holidays, spring and summer.

The current long-term plan covers **999 levels** through July 30, 2027.
