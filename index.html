ABOUT THE GAME

Puking Cat turns a familiar cat-owner problem into the whole point of the game. The cat is not sick by accident — it is trying to cause damage. Players aim the puke at furniture, food, plants, books and anything else within reach; the more objects hit, the bigger the mess and the better the score.

Levels are short and replayable with a three-star score to chase. Players earn Puke Coins and spend them on cat costumes and collectible puke palettes. New levels arrive regularly, following the calendar from autumn and Halloween through winter, spring and summer.

It is not a cat simulator and not a game about cleaning up after a pet. The joke is narrower: cats somehow puke on exactly the things you wish they would avoid. That becomes the mechanic.

WHY IT EXISTS

The idea came from real life with our cats — the sense that a cat puking on the rug, the blanket, or the one thing you least want ruined can't possibly be an accident.

It sat in Apple Notes for years before becoming a real game, built over two months of weekends for RevenueCat Shipaton 2026, with a lot of attention paid to small UI details and polish — even when the premise is ridiculous.

FOUNDER QUOTE

“We used to joke that whenever one of our cats puked, it somehow managed to land on the rug, the blanket, or whatever was hardest to clean. I wrote down the idea for a game where the cat was doing that on purpose. Years later, it finally became Puking Cat.”
